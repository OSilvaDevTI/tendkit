# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two readers, equally important:

- **Developers already building on shadcn/ui and Next.js.** They want a support inbox inside their app without a per-seat vendor, and they judge by code quality, how it fits their stack, and whether the components look like theirs. They arrive from GitHub, the shadcn registry directory or a search like "support inbox Next.js".
- **Founders and small teams leaving Zendesk or Intercom.** They're tired of seat pricing and of customer data living elsewhere. They decide; a developer implements. They need to see in seconds that it does the job and answers next to the customer's account.

## Product Purpose

Tendkit is an open-source (MIT) shadcn registry: a shared support inbox, tickets, a customer portal and channel adapters (email, SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, API) that you copy into your own app. The site (tendkit.softwaresavants.dev) has one job: make a visitor understand what it does in one second, prove it's real through a live demo, and get a developer to the install command. Success is stars, installs, and adapter contributions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, yours. Quiet confidence in the shadcn/ui tradition: the product is the hero, details are exact, nothing is hyped. The recurring message is ownership: it's your code, your components, your database, your design system.

## Anti-references

- Zendesk / Intercom marketing: stock photos of smiling agents, enterprise blue, chat-bubble illustrations.
- Generic AI SaaS templates: gradient blobs, glass cards, big-number stat rows, identical icon-card grids.
- Heavy dark "dev tool" aesthetic: neon on black, terminal cosplay.

## Design Principles

1. **Native to shadcn/ui.** It should look like it belongs on ui.shadcn.com: neutral, exact, components on stage. If it wouldn't sit naturally next to shadcn's own pages, it's wrong.
2. **Prove ownership by restyling.** Customizability is shown, not claimed: the visitor changes the theme and the real inbox follows.
3. **Show the product doing its job.** Real screens and real flows over illustrations; every visual tells "a message becomes a ticket next to the customer's account".
4. **Honest and exact.** Every claim matches the code; no invented numbers or logos.
5. **Developers and deciders, same page.** Plain outcome language up top, the install command one scroll away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast in both themes, visible focus, keyboard-operable theme switcher and demo. The hero loop respects prefers-reduced-motion with a meaningful still frame. The support UI already supports right-to-left (Arabic) and should keep doing so.
